Swaps where BOX has every GBO leg: GBO vs BOX financial status

Scope: NY SCH (branch 20007.4), interest rate swaps, process date 25-Sep-2026, PRE-PARALELO (IGBOUSIB).

Population: the 81 trades (162 legs) that are in both systems and for which BOX holds every leg GBO holds. The other 1,475 trades in both systems are left out here: 1,463 because BOX received only one leg from the Data Lake, 12 because they have a different leg structure.

Method:

- Compared tables: GBO DEVENG.T_PGT_IRFINANCST_S vs BOX BOX_FE.T_BOX_IRFINANCST_S.
- Matched per leg on FRONT_ID + direction (FK_DIRECTION) + currency (FK_CURRENCY).
- Every column both tables have is compared; amounts within 0.01. Columns only GBO has (CARRY*, MARKETVALUE*, BPV) aren’t compared.
- BOX’s all-zero USD settlement-currency rows (its modelling of non-deliverable swaps) are left out.
Headline

BOX reproduces GBO’s financial status exactly wherever it receives the trade’s cash flows.

- 46 of the 81 trades (57%) match on every compared amount.
- 29 differ only because BOX received no dated cash flows for them, so its “paid to date” columns are 0.
- 6 trades are real BOX-side questions: 3 on fee P&L, 3 on notional.
